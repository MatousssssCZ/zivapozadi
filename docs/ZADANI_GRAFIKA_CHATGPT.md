# Grafika, kterou má dodat ChatGPT

Ke každému zadání přilož **pozadí náměstí** (`pozadi-radnice.png`) a **arch dílů dospělé postavy**
(přední i zadní 3/4 pohled) jako stylovou předlohu. Vždy chtěj skutečně průhledné PNG, bez textu,
bez stínu na zemi a ve 2× rozlišení (kvůli 4K). Pořadí = priorita.

## 1. Dětská postava (arch dílů) – P1

Odemkne: rodič s dítětem u stromku, chytání vloček, koulovačku dětí, sněhuláka s dítětem, sáňky.

```text
Create a production sheet of separate painted body parts for a CHILD character (about 7 years old,
1.2 m tall, slightly larger head relative to body) that exactly matches the attached adult parts sheet
in style: soft painterly brushwork, cool blue-lilac ambient light with gentle warm highlights, muted
winter clothing colours, soft outlines. Two views, each in its own half of the sheet: front three-quarter
(facing right) and back three-quarter (facing right, seen from behind). For each view draw every part
separately with generous transparent space between parts: head with hair, winter hat with pompom,
scarf, torso in a short winter coat, upper arm, forearm, mitten hand, thigh, shin, boot. Every part must
have rounded overlapping ends at the joints so it can be rotated in 2D. Same scale ratio between parts
as a real child. Neutral straight pose, no ground shadow, true transparent background, no text, no labels,
same lighting direction as the adult sheet.
```

## 2. Póza dřepu / předklonu – P1

Odemkne: nabírání sněhu při koulovačce, zvedání upadlé rukavice, zvedání koulí sněhuláka.

```text
Using the attached adult parts sheet as the exact style and colour reference, paint additional
separate parts for a CROUCHING / bending pose: a torso in the same winter coat bent forward about 15
degrees with a shorter, slightly bunched coat hem (so the coat does not cover bent knees), and a thigh
and shin pair drawn for a deeply bent knee (about 100 degrees). Front three-quarter and back
three-quarter view, facing right. Separate parts, rounded overlapping joint ends, transparent
background, no ground shadow, no text. Then do the same set for the attached CHILD sheet.
```

## 3. Telefon – P1

Odemkne: focení u stromku, kontrolu telefonu při čekání.

```text
Paint a small smartphone as a separate 2D prop matching the attached winter painting style (soft
brushwork, cool ambient light, a small soft warm reflection). Two images side by side: the front
(dark screen with a faint glow, rounded corners) and the back (dark grey case with a small camera).
Upright, straight-on, no hand, no shadow, transparent background, no text or logos. About 70 x 140 px
each in the final sheet, painted at double resolution.
```

## 4. Rukavice a hadřík – P1

Odemkne: upadlou rukavici, otírání pultu prodavačem.

```text
Paint two small separate props in the style of the attached character sheet: (1) a single knitted
winter mitten lying on snow-free ground seen from slightly above, plus the same mitten hanging as if
held between two fingers; (2) a folded cream kitchen cloth and the same cloth slightly crumpled in a
hand-held shape. Soft painterly look, cool ambient light, transparent background, no hands, no
shadows, no text.
```

## 5. Lopata, odhrnutá dlažba a hromada sněhu – P2

Odemkne: odklízení sněhu.

```text
Paint, in the style of the attached snowy town-square painting, three separate transparent assets:
(1) a snow shovel with a wooden handle and a dark metal blade, drawn so the handle and blade could be
held with two hands, plus a separate small lump of snow lying on the blade; (2) a horizontal strip of
cleared cobblestone pavement about 3 m long and 1 m deep as seen from a camera 2.6 m high about 30 m
away (very flat perspective), with soft irregular snowy edges; (3) a low pile of shovelled snow about
0.5 m tall seen from the same camera. Pastel blue-lilac snow, soft brushwork, transparent background,
no shadows outside the objects, no text.
```

## 6. Pes (díly pro 2D kostru) – P2

Odemkne: venčení psa, čekání u stánku, hlazení, setkání psů, setřesení sněhu, setkání s kočkou.

```text
Create a production sheet of separate painted body parts for a medium-sized friendly dog (a
beagle-like mixed breed) for a 2D cut-out rig, matching the attached winter town-square painting and
character sheet in style: painterly, soft edges, cool blue-lilac ambient light, small warm highlights.
Two views, each in its own half of the sheet: three-quarter front (facing right) and three-quarter
back (facing right). Separate parts with transparent space between them: torso, head with muzzle and
collar (collar ring visible for the leash), left ear, right ear, tail, front upper leg, front lower leg
with paw, hind upper leg, hind lower leg with paw. Rounded overlapping joint ends so parts can rotate.
Neutral standing pose, no clothes, no Christmas hat, no ground shadow, true transparent background,
no text. Add a second small sheet with the head lowered for sniffing and the dog sitting (torso and
folded hind legs as separate parts).
```

## 7. Kočka (díly pro 2D kostru) – P2

Odemkne: kočku procházející náměstím, pozorující, čistící se a vyskakující na sokl.

```text
Create a production sheet of separate painted body parts for a slender tabby cat for a 2D cut-out
rig, matching the attached winter painting and character sheet in style (painterly, soft edges, cool
blue-lilac ambient light, small warm highlights). Two views: three-quarter front and three-quarter
back, both facing right. Separate parts with transparent space: torso, head, left ear, right ear, tail
split into three segments, front upper leg, front lower leg with paw, hind upper leg, hind lower leg
with paw. Rounded overlapping joint ends. Also separate parts for a SITTING cat (torso and folded hind
legs) and a crouched-before-jump torso. No collar, no ground shadow, transparent background, no text.
```

## 8. Sáňky a sedící dítě – P2

Odemkne: dospělého, který táhne dítě na sáňkách.

```text
Paint, in the style of the attached character sheets, a wooden children's sledge seen in three-quarter
front view facing right: separate layers for the runners, the seat, and a front rope attachment point,
plus a separate rope piece. Then paint the CHILD from the child sheet in a SITTING pose for the sledge:
separate torso (upright), thighs horizontal, shins angled down, so the child can be placed on the seat
with the sledge front runner drawn over the feet. Transparent background, no ground shadow, no text.
```

## 9. Nové stánky ve vrstvách – P2

Odemkne: přirozené zasazení stánků (prodavač stojí celý uvnitř a zakrývá ho pult). Podrobné prompty
pro všechny čtyři stánky a pro rozdělení do vrstev jsou v `docs/zdroje/zadani-zasazeni-stanku.md`
(kapitola A.5–A.7). Začni nápojovým stánkem:

```text
Create ONE painterly hot-drink Christmas stall as an isolated layered-scene master, using the attached
snowy Liberec square painting as the main style and lighting reference. Physical scale: 3.2 m roof
width, 1.8 m depth, 2.65 m ridge height, 1.05 m serving counter; camera 2.58 m high about 33 m away,
almost front-on, no top-down view. Square transparent 1536x1536 canvas with margins, front ground
centre at (768,1376), roof x128..1408, ridge y316, eaves y556, counter front edge y956 (invisible
guides). Brass samovar on the rear LEFT counter, cups and cocoa tins on the rear RIGHT shelves, empty
centre for a full-height animated vendor. Cool blue-lilac light on the exterior, localized warm amber
light inside, no uniform orange grading. No people, no ground, no cast shadow, no outer glow, no text.
```

Pak ze schváleného obrázku (bez přemalování): přední část (`front.png`) – střecha, sloupky, pult;
zadní část (`back.png`) – zadní stěna a police s doplněným skrytým interiérem; obojí na stejném
plátně a ve stejné poloze.

## 10. Části pozadí pro dveře, okna a výzdobu – P2

Odemkne: otevírání dveří radnice se světlem, rozsvěcování oken, pohyb zavěšené výzdoby.

```text
Using the attached town-square painting, create separate transparent layers on the SAME canvas size
and position as the original: (1) the main town-hall door wing alone; (2) the dark interior behind the
open door with warm light; (3) a version of the background where the door area is painted closed and
neutral so it can be covered; (4) for 6 chosen windows, a warm lit version and a darker unlit version
of each window only; (5) the hanging garlands on the stalls as separate cut-out pieces. Keep every
pixel aligned with the original painting, do not repaint anything else, no text.
```

## 11. Nepovinné vylepšení

  lépe ladily: „three painted snowballs of different sizes, a carrot nose, coal eyes and buttons,
  a short red knitted scarf, separate pieces, transparent background, soft painterly style“.
* Obláčky páry a kouře – „six soft painterly translucent steam/smoke puff sprites, white to pale
  grey, irregular edges, transparent background“.
